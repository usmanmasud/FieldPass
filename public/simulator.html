FieldPass

Phone simulator - USSD, voice and SMS without a real phone
HomeDashboard

Each phone calls this instance's real /ussd, /voice and /sms endpoints, as Africa's Talking would. The inbox shows every SMS, airtime top-up and call sent to that number. Demo: the farmer registers and adds a field, then opens the stalks. The herder gets an alert and books. The farmer enters the pass code and both receive airtime.
