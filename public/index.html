FieldPass

Burtali - harvest stalks for peaceful grazing
DashboardPhone simulator

Harvest leftovers become grazing deals, not fights.

Farmers mark which fields are still standing and which are harvested. Herders hear where they can graze and where to stay out. Leftover stalks are traded for a fee, for manure, or for free, with a pass code for every herd. It works on any phone through USSD, voice calls and SMS.

Try it on the simulatorOpen dashboard

Farmers - USSD

Dial *384*47704#

Register fields, mark them harvested, open stalks to herds with your terms, confirm the herd left with its pass code, report damage. Hausa or English.

Herders - Voice

Call and press a ward code

Hear how many fields are open and which standing fields to avoid. Press 1 to book. The pass code is read out and sent by SMS.

Everyone - SMS

Alerts and pass codes

Herders get alerts when fields open on their route. Mediators (Ardo, village head, ADP officer) are alerted the moment damage is reported.

Airtime

Paid for keeping the peace

Farmers earn airtime for timely harvest updates. Both sides earn airtime when a deal closes with no damage.
